# @deckio/deck-engine v1.19.1

**Bug fix** — the in-deck hide/delete controls (`SlideEditTools`) could latch
disabled after a successful op, so you couldn't toggle a slide back from inside the
deck (only from the launcher thumbnails).

## What was wrong

After a successful hide/delete, the controls kept their optimistic `busy` lock,
assuming the deck.config.js write would always trigger a **full Vite reload** that
remounts the component and resets the lock. But hiding the **current** slide usually
lands as a **state-preserving HMR update** instead: the `hiddenSlides` prop changes
and the slide dims, but nothing remounts — so `busy` stayed `true` forever and the
eye/trash buttons stayed disabled. Unhiding from the launcher thumbnail still worked
because that button's state lives in the launcher (parent), untouched by the deck's
HMR.

## Fix

`SlideEditTools` now snapshots `hidden` / `totalSlides` when an op starts and releases
the `busy` lock as soon as the deck reflects the change — whether that arrives as a
full reload (component remounts) or an HMR update (props change in place), and
regardless of whether the fetch response or the HMR event lands first. A timeout
safety net guarantees the controls can never stay latched.

## Validation

- Engine test suite: **361 passing**.
- Manually verified: hide → slide dims → eye button immediately re-enables and toggles
  back to show, from inside the deck (no reliance on the launcher thumbnail).
